Wholesale

Coffee for your café, restaurant or office.

We roast for about thirty accounts, from three-seat espresso bars in Black Mountain to the dining room at a 120-cover restaurant downtown. Every one of them has our cell numbers.

What you get

  • Coffee roasted for your menu. We will help you choose an espresso and a drip coffee, and we can develop a house blend once you are ordering 40 pounds a week or more.
  • Training for your staff. Two on-site sessions when you open with us, then a refresher whenever you hire new baristas. Our training bar at the roastery is yours to use by appointment.
  • Equipment help. We do not lease machines, but we will recommend grinders and espresso machines that fit your volume and introduce you to the technicians we trust.
  • Delivery. Free delivery twice a week within 40 miles of Asheville. Further out, we ship by UPS Ground at cost.

Pricing

Wholesale pricing is per pound and depends on the coffee and your weekly volume. As a guide:

Weekly volumeHouse blendSingle origins
10–24 lb$11.50 / lb$13.00–$16.50 / lb
25–59 lb$10.75 / lb$12.25–$15.75 / lb
60 lb and up$10.00 / lb$11.50–$15.00 / lb

Coffee comes in 5 lb bags. There is no contract; we ask for 30 days’ notice if you plan to switch roasters.

Getting started

Tell us a little about your shop and we will bring samples by (or mail them, if you are outside the area) and set up a tasting with your team.
