The Hollowbrook subscription

Fresh coffee on your doorstep, as often as you drink it.

$19 per 12 oz bag, shipping included. Every one, two or four weeks. No contracts — skip a delivery, pause for a month or cancel from your account.

How it works

1. Choose your coffee

Roaster’s choice rotates through whatever is tasting best, so you will drink four or five origins a season. Or pick one coffee and we will send it every time.

2. Pick a schedule

One, two or three bags, every one, two or four weeks. Most two-person households land on one bag every two weeks.

3. We roast and ship

Your coffee is roasted on Tuesday or Friday and leaves the building the same afternoon. You get a tracking email and a card with brewing notes.

Questions

Is shipping really included?

Yes, anywhere in the contiguous United States. We do not currently ship subscriptions to Alaska, Hawaii or outside the US.

Can I give a subscription as a gift?

Gift subscriptions run for three, six or twelve months and are paid up front. Email us with the recipient’s address and we will include a handwritten card in the first box.

What if I am going on vacation?

Reply to any shipping email or write to us and we will skip or pause your deliveries. There is no fee and no minimum number of deliveries.
