Wholesale for restaurants

Coffee as good as the rest of your menu.

We roast small batches in West Asheville, help you pick a coffee that suits your food and train your team to serve it well, from the first cup at brunch to the last one after dessert.

Who this is for

Restaurants, bistros and dining rooms in Western North Carolina that care where their ingredients come from and want the coffee to keep up with the kitchen.

Sound familiar?

  • “We source everything else locally, then serve coffee nobody thought twice about.”
  • “Front of house turns over and the espresso drifts every time.”
  • “I don’t have time to manage another supplier.”

How we help

  • Coffee chosen for your menu. We will taste with you and suggest an espresso and a drip coffee that sit well alongside your food. Once you are ordering 40 pounds a week or more, we can develop a house blend.
  • Coffee you can talk about. We buy from farms and importers we know by name, so your servers can tell guests where the coffee came from and how it was processed.
  • Training for your staff. Two on-site sessions when you start with us, then a refresher whenever you bring on new staff. Our training bar at the roastery is yours to use by appointment.
  • Equipment help. We do not lease machines, but we will recommend grinders and espresso machines that fit your volume and introduce you to the technicians we trust.
  • One less thing to chase. Free delivery twice a week within 40 miles of Asheville, in 5 lb bags. Further out, we ship by UPS Ground at cost.

Pricing is per pound and depends on the coffee and your weekly volume; there is a price guide on our wholesale page. No contract, just 30 days’ notice if you plan to switch roasters.

Restaurants that pour Hollowbrook

Our wholesale accounts run from three-seat espresso bars in Black Mountain to the dining room at a 120-cover restaurant downtown. Every one of them has our cell numbers.

Let’s taste some coffee

Tell us about your restaurant and we will bring samples by (or mail them, if you are outside the area) and set up a tasting with your team.
