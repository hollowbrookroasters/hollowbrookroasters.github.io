Wholesale for offices

Fresh coffee for your office.

Your team drinks coffee roasted in small batches in West Asheville, delivered on roast days. We help you pick the coffee and the equipment, and show everyone how to brew it.

Who this is for

Offices, studios and workplaces in Western North Carolina where the coffee pot is the busiest spot in the building, and whoever orders the coffee would like one less thing to worry about.

Sound familiar?

  • “Half the team walks to a coffee shop because the office coffee is stale.”
  • “Nobody knows how much coffee to use, so every pot tastes different.”
  • “I order coffee in between everything else, and we keep running out.”

How we help

  • Coffee that’s actually fresh. We roast every Tuesday and Friday and deliver on roast days, so the coffee in your break room is days old, not months.
  • A coffee your team will like. We will help you pick a drip coffee (and an espresso, if you have a machine). Our house blend, Blue Ridge Breakfast, is a good place to start.
  • A simple way to brew it well. We will show your team how to brew it, and our training bar at the roastery is yours to use by appointment.
  • Equipment advice. We do not lease machines, but we will recommend brewers, grinders and espresso machines that fit your office and introduce you to the technicians we trust.
  • Steady, easy delivery. Free delivery twice a week within 40 miles of Asheville, in 5 lb bags. Further out, we ship by UPS Ground at cost.

Pricing is per pound and depends on the coffee and your weekly volume; there is a price guide on our wholesale page. No contract, just 30 days’ notice if you plan to switch roasters.

Offices that pour Hollowbrook

We roast for about thirty cafés, restaurants and offices across Western North Carolina, and every one of them has our cell numbers.

Let’s taste some coffee

Tell us how many people you are brewing for and we will bring samples by (or mail them, if you are outside the area) so your team can taste before you decide.
