Wholesale for cafés

Good coffee for your café, made simple.

We roast your espresso and drip coffee in small batches in West Asheville, train your baristas and help you dial in. You get on with running your café.

Who this is for

Independent cafés and espresso bars in Western North Carolina, from a three-seat bar to a busy neighborhood shop, that want coffee from a roaster they can call by name.

Sound familiar?

  • “The espresso tasted great last month. Now it’s sour and nobody can tell me why.”
  • “I just hired two new baristas and there’s no time to train them properly.”
  • “Our coffee shows up whenever it shows up, and it’s been sitting in a warehouse.”

How we help

  • Fresh, consistent coffee. We roast on a 12-kilo Probat every Tuesday and Friday and deliver on roast days.
  • An espresso and a drip coffee that fit your menu. We will help you choose both, and we can develop a house blend once you are ordering 40 pounds a week or more.
  • Training for your baristas. Two on-site sessions when you open with us, then a refresher whenever you hire new staff. Our training bar at the roastery is yours to use by appointment.
  • Dial-in and equipment help. We do not lease machines, but we will recommend grinders and espresso machines that fit your volume and introduce you to the technicians we trust.
  • Delivery on roast days. Free delivery twice a week within 40 miles of Asheville. Further out, we ship by UPS Ground at cost.

Pricing is per pound and depends on the coffee and your weekly volume; there is a price guide on our wholesale page. No contract, just 30 days’ notice if you plan to switch roasters.

Cafés that pour Hollowbrook

We roast for about thirty cafés, restaurants and offices across Western North Carolina, and every one of them has our cell numbers.

Let’s taste some coffee

Tell us a little about your café and we will bring samples by (or mail them, if you are outside the area) and set up a tasting with your team.
