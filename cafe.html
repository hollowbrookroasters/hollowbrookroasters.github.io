The café & roastery

Come sit by the roaster.

Our coffee bar shares a 1940s brick building with the roastery on Haywood Road in West Asheville. On roast days you can watch every batch drop from the counter.

Find us

1047 Haywood Road

Five minutes from downtown, between the Haywood Road post office and the river. There is free parking behind the building (enter from Brevard Road) and a bike rack out front. The ART bus W1 route stops across the street.

On the menu

Drip coffee is always Blue Ridge Breakfast plus one rotating single origin. Espresso drinks are pulled on a La Marzocco Linea with whichever coffee we are most excited about that week. We also make a slow-steeped cold brew, chai from a local spice blend, and hot chocolate with Asheville-made chocolate.

Pastries come from a bakery two blocks down the road every morning; they usually sell out by early afternoon on weekends.

Cuppings

On the first Saturday of every month at 10am we hold a free public cupping of our newest coffees. No experience needed and no need to book; just show up a few minutes early.
